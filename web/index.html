Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Everything works on-device. Signing in is optional and only adds backup & travel buddies.
  • Does it work offline? Yes — plans and reminders work in airplane mode.
  • How do I delete my data? Settings → Delete all data (device) and Settings → Delete account & backup (server, if you signed in).
  • Flight number search finds nothing? Double-check the date — codeshares resolve to the operating airline automatically.

Privacy

Short version: no account required, nothing leaves your phone unless you opt in. The full policy lives at reclock privacy.
