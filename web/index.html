Support

Questions, answered.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works on-device with no sign-in. Signing in with Apple is optional and only adds encrypted backup and travel buddies.

Does it really work offline?

Yes. Your plan and its reminders are generated and scheduled on your phone, so they fire even in airplane mode at 38,000 feet.

Is this medical advice?

No — Reclock offers general wellness guidance for travel, grounded in circadian science. If you have a sleep disorder or health condition, talk to a clinician.

Flight-number search finds nothing?

Double-check the departure date. Codeshares resolve to the operating airline automatically, so search by the flight you're actually on.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all data wipes the device. If you signed in, Settings → Delete account & backup removes the server copy permanently, too.

Something's broken or you have an idea?

Open an issue on GitHub — it goes straight to the maker.
